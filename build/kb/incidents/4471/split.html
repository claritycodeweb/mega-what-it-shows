The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so 7 April comes out as 4 July. App, email and form bookings on booking-v2 all go through it. This is the bug the queued fix is for.

hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what the hint tells them, month first, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. Only form bookings at booking-v3 tenants. Fixing the parser does not fix this: the hint is wrong, not the reading.

one-day: the date moves by one day. No day and month swap here: Tuesday 12 May becomes Monday 11 May, a meeting lands on Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto. That points at time zones or the day boundary, not at the parser. The desk saw this (desk-notes.json) and files these under parser by tenant, so they count there too; the time-zone fix is separate.
